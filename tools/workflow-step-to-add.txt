Add these two steps to .github/workflows/main.yml, between "Validate before
publishing" and "Rebuild pages". They must be pasted through GitHub's web
editor - pushing to .github/workflows/ needs a token with the workflow scope.

      - name: Watch NZ media for lottery topics
        run: python3 tools/watch_news.py || echo "news watch skipped"

      - name: Write any notable results into news
        run: python3 tools/auto_news.py || echo "auto-news skipped"

Both end with "|| echo ..." on purpose: neither is allowed to fail the run.
If the news feed is unreachable or the editorial step errors, results must
still publish.
