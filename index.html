What is Keno NZ?

If you’re a fan of the classic lottery, then you’re definitely at the right place. Keno NZ is a numerical lottery that has a lot in common with bingo or lotto. The rules of the game are extremely simple: you have to choose a number between 1 and 80, and then the winning numbers are drawn at random and notified to players. The winnings directly depend on how many matches you get in total.

How to play Keno NZ?

In general, Keno NZ is quite easy to play, as the rules are quite simple:

  1. Select the number of numbers (usually from 1 to 10).
  2. Set the bet amount.
  3. Wait for the draw – the lottery will randomly determine the winning numbers.
  4. Compare your numbers with the Keno NZ results – the more matches, the higher the winnings.
