Powerball rolls again as three tickets share Lotto's top prize

Draw 2617 produced three First Division Lotto winners taking $333,333 each, but Powerball went unstruck for another draw, carrying its prize pool past $25.4 million.

Three Lotto tickets shared First Division in Wednesday night's draw, while Powerball went unclaimed for a further draw and Strike also rolled over.

The result

Lotto draw 2617 was made on Wednesday 2 September 2026 at 8:20pm. The winning line was 4, 8, 22, 25, 32 and 38, with 34 drawn as the bonus ball. The Powerball number was 3, and the Strike sequence came out as 32, 38, 4, 25.

Three tickets matched all six main numbers, splitting a First Division pool of $999,999 for $333,333 each. No ticket held the Powerball number alongside a First Division line, so the Powerball top prize rolled over.

Powerball and Strike both roll

Powerball's First Division found no winner, leaving its prize pool sitting at $25,492,796. The game still paid out across its lower divisions, distributing $492,796 — four tickets took Second Division at $5,537 each, rising to a combined $19,630 when their Lotto winnings were included.

Strike also went unstruck at the top. Strike Four produced no winner from a pool of $668,179, though 77 tickets landed Strike Three for $707 apiece and 4,223 took Strike Two at $72. Across all Strike divisions, 113,984 tickets won something.

Reading the numbers

A shared First Division is not unusual. Lotto's top division is won by matching six numbers from 40, and when multiple tickets carry the same line the prize is split between them — which is why three winners here took a third each rather than the full pool.

Powerball works differently, and is why the headline figure keeps climbing. It requires a First Division Lotto line plus the separately drawn Powerball number, so it is struck far less often. Each draw without a winner carries the pool forward.

Worth noting for anyone reading a rolling jackpot as a signal: a larger pool does not improve the chance of winning it. The odds on any given line are unchanged from one draw to the next, whatever the prize has grown to.

The scale of the lower divisions is easy to overlook next to a seven-figure headline. Division Seven alone returned a bonus ticket to 289,391 entries — more than five in six of all winning tickets in the draw — while the three First Division winners represent a vanishingly small fraction of the 480,746 tickets that won something across the three games.
