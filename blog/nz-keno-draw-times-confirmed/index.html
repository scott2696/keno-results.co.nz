NZ Keno draw times, confirmed across 56 days

Four draws a day at 10:01am, 1:01pm, 3:01pm and 6:01pm - exactly 55 draws at each time across our archive, weekends and holidays included.

New Zealand Keno draws four times a day. That much is widely stated. What is harder to find is confirmation that the times hold consistently — including weekends and public holidays.

Our archive covers 56 consecutive days, 12 July to 5 September 2026, and contains 220 draws. Sorted by time of day, they fall into exactly four groups:

NZ Keno draws by time of day
DrawTime (NZ)Draws recorded
Morning10:01am55
Midday1:01pm55
Afternoon3:01pm55
Evening6:01pm55

Fifty-five at each, with no gaps and no extras. Broken down by weekday the counts sit between 29 and 32 per day of the week — the variation is simply how many of each weekday fall inside a 56-day span, not a difference in schedule. Saturdays and Sundays draw the same as Tuesdays.

What it means for a limit

Four draws a day is roughly 28 a week and about 1,460 a year. Keno's odds are fixed per draw, so playing more draws does not improve them — it multiplies how often you are exposed to the same fixed house edge.

That is the practical argument for setting any limit per day or per week rather than per draw. A limit that resets four times a day is not really a limit. More on that on our responsible gambling page.

A caveat

These times are observed, not a schedule published to us. Lotto NZ set the draw calendar and can change it. If they do, this page will be out of date before we are told — the timestamp on the latest draw is always the authoritative answer to when the last one happened.
