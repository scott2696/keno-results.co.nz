What 220 draws actually say about hot numbers

We hold every Keno draw since 12 July. Widening the window from five draws to 220 collapses the gap between the hottest and coldest number from 100 points to 15 - which is randomness becoming visible.

Every results site publishes hot and cold numbers. Almost none of them show you the one thing that makes the data honest: what happens to the gap as you widen the window.

We hold 220 confirmed draws, from 12 July to 5 September 2026. Because 20 of 80 numbers come out each draw, every number should appear about 25% of the time over a long enough run. Here is how far the extremes sit from that baseline at each window size.

220 draws33.6%18.2%15.5 pp

Over the last five draws, one number appeared in every single one and several appeared in none. On a hot-and-cold widget that looks like a screaming signal. It is not. It is what five samples of a random process look like.

Stretch the same data to 220 draws and the hottest number sits at 33.6% and the coldest at 18.2% — both drifting toward 25%, exactly as they should. Nothing changed about the game. Only the sample size changed.

The numbers, for the record

Across all 220 draws, the most-drawn numbers were 41 (74 times), 66 (69) and 19 (68). The least drawn were 37 (40), 48 (41) and 56 (41). The expected count for any number is 55.

Is 74 unusually high? Not really. With 80 numbers sampled this many times, some will land well above expectation and some well below purely by chance — and which ones do it is different every time you look.

Why we still publish it

Because it is a genuine record of what happened, and because the control itself is the argument. Open our hot and cold page and move the window from 5 draws to 250. Watching the extremes converge is the clearest demonstration we can offer that short-run streaks carry no information about the next draw.

A number that has come up in 80% of the last five draws has exactly the same 25% chance next draw as one that has not appeared at all. The draw has no memory. Neither should your ticket.
