Contact us

Corrections, questions and enquiries all reach the same place. Tell us which one it is and we can answer faster.

Before you write

We are an independent results service — not Lotto New Zealand. We cannot check a ticket officially, pay a prize, look up your account, or resolve anything to do with a purchase. For any of that, contact Lotto NZ directly.

Report a wrong result

This is the one we most want to hear about, and we treat it as urgent. Include the draw number and what you expected to see, and we will check it against the source.

Report a result error

If we have published something materially wrong, we correct it and note the correction. See our corrections policy.

General enquiries

Questions about the site, the data, how something is calculated, or anything else.

info@keno-results.co.nz

Media and partnerships

For press enquiries, data questions, or advertising and partnership proposals, use the same address and put the topic in the subject line.

info@keno-results.co.nz

Privacy requests

To ask what personal information we hold, or to request correction, write to us with "Privacy" in the subject. In practice we hold almost nothing — see the privacy policy — but you are welcome to ask.

What to expect

  • We read everything that arrives at this address.
  • Result corrections are prioritised over everything else.
  • We are a small operation, so general enquiries may take a few days.
  • We cannot give gambling advice, tips, or predictions — and anyone who does is not being straight with you. See the odds.

Gambling harm

We are not a support service and cannot help with gambling harm. If gambling is causing problems for you or someone you know, free and confidential help is available from the Gambling Helpline Aotearoa. More on our responsible gambling page.
