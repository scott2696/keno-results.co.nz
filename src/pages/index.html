    How Keno works

    Twenty numbers are drawn at random from 1 to 80. You choose between one and ten of them — your spots — and your prize depends on how many you match.

    What are the odds?

    On a six-spot ticket, matching all six happens about once in 7,753 tickets. We publish the arithmetic rather than rounded marketing figures.
