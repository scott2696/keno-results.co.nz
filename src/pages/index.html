    About these Keno results

    Keno in NZ draws four times every day, including weekends and public holidays, at 10:01am, 1:01pm, 3:01pm and 6:01pm NZ time. The panel at the top of this page always holds the latest Keno results — whichever draw ran most recently — so the 6pm Keno results appear here from just after 6:01pm each evening. Daily Keno results going back to our first recorded draw are kept in the full archive, each draw on its own page, so a result from last month is as easy to look up as the Keno numbers today.

    Numbers come from Lotto NZ’s own published results. Nothing is typed in by hand, and every draw has to be exactly twenty unique numbers between 1 and 80 before it is shown — if a draw fails that check it is discarded rather than published. That is why the archive here is smaller than some others and why we would rather show nothing than show a number we cannot stand behind. Our data and validation page explains the whole process.

    You cannot buy a ticket here — this site only publishes results. To play Keno online you go through MyLotto, Lotto NZ’s own site, or any Lotto retailer; our where to play page covers both, and Keno prizes explains how a win is worked out. Keno is one of four Lotto NZ games drawn to a fixed schedule, alongside Lotto, Powerball and Bullseye. We publish the latest result for each. If you are new to the game, the how to play guide covers spots, multipliers and prize divisions, and the odds tables give the real probability of every outcome, computed from the rules rather than rounded for marketing.

    How Keno works

    Twenty numbers are drawn at random from 1 to 80. You choose between one and ten of them — your spots — and your prize depends on how many you match.

    What are the odds?

    On a six-spot ticket, matching all six happens about once in 7,753 tickets. We publish the arithmetic rather than rounded marketing figures.
