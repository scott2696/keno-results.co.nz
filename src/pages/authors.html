Editorial

Authors & editorial standards

Who is behind this site, how the results are produced, and the rules everything published here has to pass.

How results are produced

Draw results on this site are not written by a person. They are read automatically from Lotto NZ's published results shortly after each draw, normalised, and validated before publication. No one types a number in, which removes the most common source of error on results sites.

Every draw must pass all of the following or it is discarded rather than published:

  • Exactly 20 numbers.
  • Every number an integer between 1 and 80.
  • No duplicates within a draw.
  • A valid draw identifier and an ISO 8601 timestamp.
  • No duplicate draw identifiers in the feed.

The check runs twice — once when the data is fetched, before anything is written, and again in your browser before anything is drawn on screen. It is public: it lives in tools/validate_draws.py in this site's repository. See our data and validation for more.

How the written pages are produced

The guide pages — how to play, odds, FAQs, the multiplier, rules — are written and reviewed by a person, and follow the standards below.

Our standards

  • Odds are calculated, not quoted. Every probability on this site is computed from the rules of the game using the hypergeometric formula, which is published on the odds page so you can check any figure yourself.
  • We do not publish prize amounts. Payouts depend on stake, multiplier and the operator's current schedule. Publishing a figure we cannot guarantee would be worse than publishing none.
  • We do not claim any system works. No selection method changes Keno's odds. Content implying otherwise is not published here, and the previous version of this site's "strategy" page was retired for exactly that reason.
  • Statistics are described, never predicted. Frequency data says what happened. It says nothing about the next draw, and our statistics page is built to demonstrate that rather than obscure it.
  • Sources are named and timestamped. Every set of numbers carries a provenance strip showing where it came from and exactly when we retrieved it.
  • Corrections are published, not quietly patched. See our corrections policy.

Editorial independence

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Lotto New Zealand or any gambling operator. We do not sell tickets, take stakes, or receive commission when anyone plays. No commercial arrangement affects what is published on the results or odds pages.

If advertising appears on this site it will be visually distinct from editorial content and marked as such, and it will never sit inside or beside a set of draw numbers where it could be mistaken for data.

Reporting an error

If something here is wrong, please tell us — include the draw number and what you expected. We would rather be corrected than be confidently wrong. Contact us.
