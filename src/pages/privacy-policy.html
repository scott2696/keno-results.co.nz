Privacy policy

Short version: we collect as little as possible, and your Keno numbers never leave your browser.

Your ticket numbers

The numbers you enter into the ticket checker are processed entirely in your browser. They are stored in your device's local storage so they are still there when you come back, and they are never transmitted to us, logged, or shared with anyone. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What we collect

This site is served as static files. We do not require an account, we have no login, and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking. We measure how many people read each page, but we do so without identifying anyone — see Analytics below. Standard web server request logs may record your IP address, the page requested and your browser's user agent, as is normal for any website.

Cookies

We set no tracking cookies. Your theme preference (light or dark) and your saved numbers are kept in local storage rather than cookies, and are only ever read by this site on your own device.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand which pages are useful and where readers come from. It sets no cookies, uses no fingerprinting, gives you no identifier, does not store your IP address, and cannot follow you to any other site.

Because it assigns no identifier, the data is not personal data about you: we can see that a page was read, roughly from where and on what kind of device, but we cannot pick you out of it, recognise you on a later visit, or link anything back to a person. We chose it over the better-known analytics products for exactly that reason. Blocking the script changes nothing about how the site works. Full details are in our cookie policy.

Third parties

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the analytics script is loaded from Cloudflare, so your browser makes a request to each to fetch those files. Nothing else third-party runs on these pages.

Some pages carry clearly labelled paid links to gambling operators. Nothing loads from those companies while you are on this site, and they learn nothing about you unless you follow a link to them, at which point their own privacy policy applies.

Children

This site concerns a gambling product and is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask what personal information we hold about you and request correction. In practice we hold essentially none, but you are welcome to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
