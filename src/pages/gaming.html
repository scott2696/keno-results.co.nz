The short version

Gambling in New Zealand runs on a simple principle: it is prohibited unless the law specifically allows it. That is the opposite of how most people assume it works. There is no general right to run a gambling operation here and then be regulated — there is a list of what is permitted, and everything outside that list is illegal.

The four things New Zealanders legally play are Lotto NZ’s games (including the Keno results this site publishes), betting through TAB NZ, gaming machines in pubs and clubs, and the six licensed land-based casinos. Online casino gambling has sat outside that structure for years, which is the gap the 2026 Act closes.
