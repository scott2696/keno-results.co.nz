Bullseye

A daily New Zealand game with a structure unlike Lotto or Keno.

How it works

Bullseye runs daily. Rather than matching a set of numbers against a larger drawn set, entries are measured against a single drawn outcome, with prize tiers based on how closely an entry matches.

How it differs from Keno

Keno gives you control over how many numbers you play, and your prize ladder follows from that choice. Bullseye has a fixed structure — there is no spot count to decide. It also draws once daily rather than on Keno's more frequent schedule.

Odds

As with every game here, the odds are fixed by the game's design and are not affected by previous results. We do not publish Bullseye odds tables, because we do not hold a verified feed for the game — Lotto New Zealand publish the current figures.

Scope

This page is background context. This site publishes verified results for Keno only; for Bullseye results and prize details, go to the official operator.
