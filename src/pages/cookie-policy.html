Cookie policy

The short version: we set no cookies at all, and nothing here identifies you or follows you to another site.

We do not use cookies

This site sets no cookies — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for functionality. That includes our audience measurement, which is described below and is deliberately cookie-free.

You may still see a short notice the first time you visit. It is there to tell you what is stored on your device, not to ask permission — there is nothing here to consent to, and dismissing it does not switch anything on or off. If we ever add something that genuinely requires consent, that notice will change into a real choice, and this page will say so before it goes live.

What we do store on your device

Two things are kept in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device and is readable only by this site:

What this site stores locally
KeyWhat it holdsWhy
picksThe numbers you entered in the ticket checkerSo your numbers are still there when you come back
themeLight or dark, if you chose oneSo the site opens the way you left it

Neither is transmitted anywhere. We cannot read them. Clearing your browser data removes both, and the site works perfectly well without them.

How we count visits

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people read and where they arrive from. We picked it over the more common alternatives for one reason: it does its job without identifying you.

It sets no cookies, uses no fingerprinting, assigns you no identifier, and cannot follow you to any other website. It records a page view with the referring site, rough country, and general device type — and it does not store your IP address. Because there is no identifier, we cannot tell a returning reader from a new one, and we cannot single you out in the data. That is the trade we chose: we lose some detail, you keep your privacy.

There is nothing here to consent to, which is why you get a notice rather than a banner with buttons. If you would rather not be counted at all, any tracker blocker will stop the script, and the site works exactly the same without it.

Third parties

Two external requests are made. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them, and the analytics script above is loaded from Cloudflare. Either may log the request for the file itself, as any server does. Nothing else third-party runs on these pages — no advertising network, no social widgets, no tag manager.

Some pages carry clearly labelled paid links to gambling operators. Those are ordinary links: nothing loads from those companies while you are here, and they learn nothing about you unless you click through to them.

If we ever add advertising that sets cookies, this page will be updated before it goes live and it will come with a proper consent mechanism.

Server logs

As with any website, the server that delivers these pages may record standard request information such as your IP address, the page requested and your browser's user agent. That is a normal part of serving a website and is not cookie-based tracking.

Managing local storage

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser's settings, usually under privacy or site data. You can also use the Clear button in the ticket checker to remove your saved numbers without touching anything else.
