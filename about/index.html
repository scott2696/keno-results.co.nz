About this site

What we publish, where it comes from, and how to tell us when we get it wrong.

What this site is

keno-results.co.nz is an independent service publishing New Zealand Keno results and explaining how the game works. We are not a gambling operator. You cannot place a bet here, and we do not take payment from visitors.

Independence

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Lotto New Zealand. They run the game and hold the official record. We report on it.

Where the numbers come from

Written content on this site follows our editorial standards, and results are produced by an automated, validated pipeline rather than typed in by hand.

Results come from MyLotto, the official Lotto New Zealand results service. We read their published results feed on a schedule — shortly after each Keno draw — and cache it here so this site loads fast and does not depend on their servers being reachable when you visit.

Every set of numbers on this site carries a provenance strip naming the source and the time we retrieved it. If we cannot verify a draw, we do not publish it: the site shows an empty state rather than a guess.

Keno draws roughly four times a day (around 10am, 1pm, 3pm and 6pm New Zealand time), and we refresh shortly after each. The timestamp on any page tells you exactly how current what you are reading is.

On the source

The feed we read is published by the operator but is not a documented public API, so it can change without notice. If it does, this site will show its unavailable state rather than stale or invented numbers. Lotto New Zealand remain the official record for every result and every prize claim.

How we validate

Before anything is published, every draw is checked against the rules of the game:

  • Exactly 20 numbers.
  • Every number an integer between 1 and 80.
  • No duplicates within a draw.
  • A valid draw identifier and timestamp.
  • No duplicate draw identifiers in the feed.

A draw failing any of these is rejected and never reaches the site. The check runs twice: once when we fetch, before anything is written, and again in your browser before anything is drawn on screen. It is public — it lives in tools/validate_draws.py in this site's repository, and the scheduled refresh will not publish if it fails.

Why this matters here

An earlier version of this site published results containing numbers between 81 and 90 — values that cannot occur in a game drawn from 1 to 80 — while its own guide correctly described the 1–80 range. That data was wrong, and it has not been carried over. The validation above exists specifically so it cannot happen again.

Reporting an error

If something here looks wrong, please tell us. Include the draw identifier and what you expected to see. We would rather be corrected than be confidently wrong, and we will publish a note when we fix something that was materially incorrect.

Email info@keno-results.co.nz.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell tickets or accept stakes.
  • We do not publish prize amounts, because those depend on your stake and the operator's current schedule.
  • We do not offer systems, predictions, or claims that any selection method improves your odds. It does not.
  • We do not track you across sites or sell visitor data. See our privacy policy.

Responsible gambling

This site is intended for people aged 18 and over. Gambling carries a fixed house edge and, over time, returns less than it takes in.

If gambling is causing harm, free and confidential help is available from the Gambling Helpline Aotearoa.
