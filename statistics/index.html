What this can and cannot tell you

Each Keno draw is independent, and the machine has no memory of previous draws. A number that has come up in 80% of the last five draws is exactly as likely to appear in the next one as a number that has not appeared at all — both sit at 25%, every draw, always.

Short windows swing widely for that reason. Over five draws a number can easily show at 80% or 0% through ordinary chance; widen the window and everything drifts back toward 25%. Try it with the selector above — watching the spread narrow as you go from 5 draws to 250 is the clearest demonstration that these are not signals.

Presenting frequency without implying prediction is one of our editorial standards.
